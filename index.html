Yesterday's News UK creates short videos about the stories and topics people in the United Kingdom were searching for the previous day.

What the app does

The operator uses an automated production workflow to research publicly available news and search trends, prepare a short video, and upload it to the operator's own YouTube channel as a private video for manual review and publication.

Google sign-in and YouTube permissions are used only by the channel operator to authorise video uploads to that channel. This website does not offer sign-in, viewer accounts, or a service that uploads to other people's channels.
